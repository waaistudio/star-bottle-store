# Store Listing Draft

## App Name

星海回音 Starry Echo

## Subtitle

匿名漂流心事，收集溫柔回音

## Short Description

把心事放進水瓶，讓陌生人的溫暖在星海裡回到你身邊。

## Long Description

Starry Echo is a quiet anonymous companion app for moments when you want to be heard gently. Write a short worry, place it into a virtual bottle, and let it drift into a healing ocean. Another warm stranger can pick it up and reply with encouragement, a virtual hug, hot tea, or a simple reminder that you are not alone.

The experience is designed around calm visuals, user-controlled ocean white noise, anonymous display names, and visible safety tools. Reports and blocks are available from user-generated content surfaces.

Future phases will add platform-compliant virtual kindness items and physical comfort pack redemption after backend safety, purchase validation, and fulfillment workflows are complete.

## Review Notes

- User-generated content has report/block entry points.
- White noise is off by default and user-controlled.
- Paid virtual items are disabled until Phase 2 purchase validation is implemented.
- Physical gift redemption is disabled until Phase 3 fulfillment and policy work is complete.
- AI Lighthouse is disabled until backend safety handling is implemented.
