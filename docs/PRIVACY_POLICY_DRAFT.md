# Privacy Policy Draft

This is a product draft for review preparation. Have counsel review before publication.

## Data We Collect

- Anonymous user id and app profile state.
- Bottle and reply content submitted by the user.
- Reports, blocks, moderation decisions, and safety signals.
- Purchase records for virtual items when Phase 2 is enabled.
- Shipping information for physical gift redemption when Phase 3 is enabled.
- Basic diagnostics needed to keep the app reliable and safe.

## How We Use Data

- To deliver anonymous bottle and reply experiences.
- To send notifications about replies and account activity.
- To prevent spam, harassment, fraud, and unsafe content.
- To process virtual purchases and physical redemptions when enabled.
- To comply with platform, legal, and safety obligations.

## Anonymous Experience

Other users see anonymous labels such as "a warm stranger." Backend systems still keep account identifiers so reports, blocks, and safety actions can work.

## User-Generated Content

Messages may be reviewed by automated systems and human moderators when needed for safety, abuse prevention, or policy enforcement.

## Account Deletion

The production app must provide an in-app account deletion request. After deletion, personal profile data should be removed or anonymized, while limited safety and transaction records may be retained where required.

## Children And Age Rating

Anonymous social features and emotional support content require careful age-rating review before release.
