# Terms Draft

This is a product draft for review preparation. Have counsel review before publication.

## Community Rules

- Be kind and supportive.
- Do not harass, threaten, shame, or manipulate others.
- Do not share private contact information.
- Do not use the app for emergency support.
- Do not spam, farm rewards, or exploit purchases.

## Safety

Starry Echo is not a medical, therapy, legal, or emergency service. If a user is in immediate danger, they should contact local emergency services or trusted people nearby.

## AI Lighthouse

AI Lighthouse replies, when enabled, must be clearly labeled as AI-generated and are intended only as supportive, non-clinical messages.

## Virtual Items

Star bottles and star fragments, when enabled, are virtual items handled through platform-compliant in-app purchases. They have no cash value and cannot be transferred outside the app.

## Physical Gift Packs

Physical gift pack redemption, when enabled, depends on inventory, shipping availability, user eligibility, and fulfillment policies.

## Enforcement

The app may remove content, limit features, suspend accounts, or preserve safety records when necessary to enforce these terms.
